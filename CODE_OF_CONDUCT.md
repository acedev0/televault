# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, constructive, and focused on the project.
- Accept useful feedback and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Protect other people's privacy and never request or expose credentials or private media.
- Avoid harassment, threats, sexualized attention, trolling, and personal attacks.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and contributions that
do not follow this code. Serious or repeated abuse may result in a temporary or permanent ban.

Report conduct concerns privately to `Acedevmc@gmail.com`. Maintainers will respect the reporter's
privacy and respond proportionately.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1.
